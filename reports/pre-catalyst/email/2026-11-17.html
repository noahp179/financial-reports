Why

  • TELUS: Québecor's offer is C$6.35 cash plus 0.17 of a Québecor share for each TELUS share. That was worth C$19.45 on Friday, a 60% premium. Québecor's shares then fell 15%, cutting the value to about C$17.44. TELUS closed at C$13.70, 27% below that, because the offer isn't firm yet: TELUS's board says it's not enough, the regulators are a big hurdle, and Québecor still needs financing. I now give a deal about a 1 in 4 chance. That works out to about +5% overall, which isn't enough for a Buy. Under C$13.31 it would be.
  • Aurora: Organigram fell 12% after a US stock-exchange warning about its low share price. Its offer for Aurora is partly paid in Organigram shares, so it's now worth about C$6.91, 5% above Aurora's C$6.60. The risk is that Organigram keeps falling, so Hold; buy under C$6.20.
