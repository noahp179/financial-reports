Why

  • TELUS: Québecor has offered 0.17 of a Québecor share for each TELUS share, worth about C$13.10. That's up from 0.16 in June, and it is not a firm offer. TELUS's board says the price is too low and the regulatory risk is too big, but it hasn't said no. The two companies' bosses meet Nov 23. If they agree, perhaps at a slightly better ratio, TELUS could reach about C$13.87; if Québecor walks away, it likely falls back near C$11.20. The odds of a deal are only about 1 in 5, so it's a Hold. Be careful if it jumps at today's open.
  • Canaccord: CEO Dan Daviau is buying the company for C$15.27 cash, 3% above Friday's C$14.79. Part of that price comes in British pounds from a UK business sale and isn't hedged, so the final amount can move a little. The company can look for a higher bid until Dec 30, and the vote is in Q1 2027. Little upside once the stock opens near the deal price. Hold.
  • Athabasca: the proxy adviser ISS recommends voting yes. The vote is now set for Nov 27, and I raised the chance of closing to 97%.
  • Aurora: Curaleaf fell another 5%, so its rival offer is worth only about C$5.90. Organigram's offer is worth about C$7.34, 4% above C$7.05.
